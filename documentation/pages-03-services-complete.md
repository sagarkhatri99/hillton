### H1 Heading
Ayurveda & Panchakarma at Hillton

### Intro Section

**Subheading:** What Is Ayurveda?

**Body Copy:**  
Ayurveda is an ancient system of medicine (5000+ years old) that sees health as a state of balance between body, mind and environment. Rather than treating isolated symptoms, Ayurveda addresses root causes by balancing your unique constitution (Dosha).

Panchakarma is Ayurveda's most powerful healing process – a deep cleansing and rejuvenation protocol that removes accumulated toxins (Ama) and resets your system.

At Hillton, we follow classical Panchakarma principles adapted to modern life. You won't need to take months off work – we'll work with your schedule while maintaining the integrity of the process.

---

### Understanding Your Dosha

**Heading:** Three Body Types in Ayurveda

**Intro Paragraph:**  
Ayurveda recognizes that everyone has a unique constitution (Dosha). Understanding your type helps us recommend therapies that will work best for you.

---

#### Dosha 1: Vata
**Description:**  
Light, quick, creative. Tends toward anxiety, dry skin, irregular digestion, fluctuating energy.

**Imbalance Signs:** Anxiety, insomnia, constipation, joint pain, scattered thinking.

**Balancing Therapies:** Warm oils, grounding practices, consistent routines, warm foods.

---

#### Dosha 2: Pitta
**Description:**  
Fiery, focused, ambitious. Tends toward intensity, heat, quick digestion, high metabolism.

**Imbalance Signs:** Irritability, excessive heat, acidity, inflammation, skin rashes.

**Balancing Therapies:** Cooling practices, meditation, cooling herbs, moderation in work.

---

#### Dosha 3: Kapha
**Description:**  
Stable, grounded, nurturing. Tends toward steady energy, slow digestion, heaviness.

**Imbalance Signs:** Weight gain, sluggish digestion, heaviness, lethargy, attachment.

**Balancing Therapies:** Stimulating practices, warm and light foods, regular movement.

---

### Core Ayurvedic Therapies

**Heading:** Panchakarma & Ayurvedic Treatments

---

#### Therapy Group 1: Classical Panchakarma (5 Main Therapies)

**Note:** These are only recommended after detailed Ayurvedic assessment. Not everyone needs all five.

##### Vaman (Therapeutic Vomiting)
- For: Kapha-related respiratory, digestive and metabolic issues  
- Effect: Clears excess kapha and toxins from upper digestive tract  

##### Virechan (Therapeutic Purgation)
- For: Pitta-related conditions, liver issues, skin disorders  
- Effect: Clears excess pitta and removes deep-seated toxins  

##### Basti (Enema Therapy)
- For: Vata-related conditions, neurological concerns, joint pain  
- Effect: Most important treatment; balances Vata and nourishes nervous system  

##### Nasya (Nasal Administration)
- For: Headaches, sinusitis, neurological issues, eye/ear health  
- Effect: Clears toxins from head region; supports mental clarity  

##### Raktamokshan (Blood Purification)
- For: Skin disorders, inflammatory conditions, blood stasis  
- Effect: Purifies blood and removes deep toxins (rarely used, only when indicated)  

---

#### Therapy Group 2: Oleation & Fomentation (Preparation Phase)

Before Panchakarma, the body is prepared:

**Abhyanga (Oil Massage)**  
- Full-body warm oil massage using medicated oils  
- Prepares tissues, nourishes, improves circulation  
- Often repeated daily for 3–7 days before main Panchakarma  

**Swedana (Fomentation)**  
- Gentle steam or herbal sweat to open pores and prepare for cleansing  

---

#### Therapy Group 3: Post-Panchakarma Recovery & Nourishment

After cleansing, the body needs careful rebuilding:

**Shamana Therapies** (Balancing/Soothing)
- Herbal treatments  
- Dietary support  
- Rest and light movement  

**Duration:** Usually 1–2 weeks of recovery care

---

#### Therapy Group 4: Supporting Ayurvedic Therapies

**Shirodhara** (Oil Stream on Forehead)  
- Specific oil dripped in steady stream on forehead for 30–60 minutes  
- Deeply calms the nervous system, supports sleep, relieves headaches  
- Often combined with Panchakarma programs  

**Kizhi** (Herbal Bolus Therapy)  
- Warm medicinal herb bundles applied to joints and muscles  
- Reduces inflammation, relieves pain, nourishes tissue  

**Pichu** (Oil-Soaked Cloth on Specific Area)  
- Targeted nourishment and healing of specific region (e.g., spine, joints)  

**Uttar Basti & Other Specialized Treatments**  
- Targeted cleansing and healing for specific organs  

---

### Panchakarma Course Structure

**Heading:** What a Panchakarma Program Looks Like

---

### **Pre-Panchakarma Phase (Days 1–3 to 7)**

**Snehana (Oleation)**  
- Daily full-body warm oil massage (Abhyanga)  
- Internal oleation (medicated ghee or oil ingested in controlled amounts)  
- Purpose: Open channels, prepare tissues for cleansing  

**Swedana (Fomentation)**  
- Herbal steam or warm fomentation following massage  
- Purpose: Open pores, prepare for main therapies  

---

### **Main Panchakarma Phase (Days typically 7–14 or 14–21)**

The specific Panchakarma therapies (Vaman, Virechan, Basti, Nasya) are performed based on individual assessment.

**Daily Schedule (approximate):**
- 6:00 AM: Light breakfast (warm ghee, rice water, herbal drink)  
- 7:00 AM: Abhyanga (massage) – 45 min  
- 8:00 AM: Swedana (steam) – 15 min  
- 9:00 AM: Panchakarma main therapy – 30–60 min  
- 10:00 AM: Rest and light snack  
- 12:00 PM: Light lunch (easy-to-digest foods)  
- 1:00–4:00 PM: Rest, light walking, meditation  
- 4:00 PM: Tea and light snack  
- 5:00 PM: Yoga or breathing practice  
- 6:30 PM: Light dinner (easily digestible)  
- 9:00 PM: Sleep  

---

### **Post-Panchakarma Recovery Phase (Days following cleansing)**

**Shamana (Recovery)**  
- Specialized herbal treatments to rebuild and balance  
- Dietary protocol (easy-to-digest, nourishing foods)  
- Rest and minimal physical exertion  
- Meditation and gentle practices  
- Duration: Usually 3–7 days (varies based on which Panchakarma was done)  

---
